What the website does today

The website is informational, allows visitors to join the Velvet Table launch list and provides a pilot Risonix licence area. Online payments are not active. Advertising measurement through the Meta Pixel is optional and starts only after consent in the cookie banner.

Risonix customer area

To access the licence area, the authentication service provides the site with a user ID, email and, when available, display name. The email is used to find associated licences; the licence server stores only a secret-protected hash of it. Music files and acoustic fingerprints stay on the device and are not uploaded to the Kreluna website.

Velvet Table launch list

When you join, Kreluna records your email address, the selected path — customer or restaurant — and, only when provided, the restaurant name and city. The general source of the registration, such as the social platform used, is also retained.

The data is used for project updates and launch notices on the basis of the consent given in the form. It is retained until launch and no longer than 12 months from registration unless consent is renewed or deletion is requested earlier.

Meta campaign measurement

After marketing consent, Kreluna uses the Meta Pixel to measure page views and a completed Velvet Table registration through the Lead event. The event does not include the email address or other form contents. Meta may process browser identifiers and technical data under its own terms and policies. Consent can be withdrawn through “Cookie settings”.

Email enquiries

If you email Kreluna, information in the message is used to read and handle the enquiry. Do not send passwords, access tokens, confidential documents or sensitive personal data in an initial message.

Cloudflare statistics and technical data

Kreluna uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand visits, viewed pages, general referral sources, countries and website performance. According to Cloudflare, the service does not use cookies or local storage, does not create individual profiles and does not collect personal data to display analytics. The hosting provider may also process technical logs required for security, availability and maintenance. Velvet Table registrations are stored in the website infrastructure. Kreluna does not use Google Analytics; the optional Meta Pixel is described above and in the Cookie Policy.

Your rights

You may withdraw consent or request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection or portability where applicable by writing to the contacts above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority.
