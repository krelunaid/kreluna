Provisional privacy information

This page describes the website conservatively in its current state. It is not yet the final legal privacy notice: controller details and legal bases must be reviewed before commercial launch and online payments.

Legal review required. This page is published for transparency during pre-launch and is not a substitute for documentation based on the controller’s verified details.

What the website does today

The website is primarily informational. A pilot Risonix customer area using ChatGPT sign-in is active; online payments, contact forms and analytics are not active. Visitors may choose to email krelunaid@gmail.com directly.

Risonix customer area

To access the licence area, the authentication service provides the site with a user ID, email and, when available, display name. The email is used to find associated licences; the licence server stores only a secret-protected hash of it. Music files and acoustic fingerprints stay on the device and are not uploaded to the Kreluna website.

Email enquiries

If you email Kreluna, information in the message is used to read and handle the enquiry. Do not send passwords, access tokens, confidential documents or sensitive personal data in an initial message.

Technical data and hosting

The hosting provider may process technical logs needed for security, availability and maintenance under its own terms. Kreluna does not currently use Google Analytics, advertising pixels or profiling on the site.

Before services are activated

Before commercial launch, online payments or additional processing is enabled, this page must be replaced with a complete notice identifying the controller, legal bases, recipients, retention and applicable rights.
