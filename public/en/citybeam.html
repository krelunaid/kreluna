Advertising in Times Square: before you book

How much does a Times Square ad cost?

CityBeam does not yet have confirmed rates. A quote should identify the screen, spot length, number of plays, dates, time bands and included services. We will publish prices and terms only after operator confirmation.

Can I book or pay now?

Not yet. You can request information with your destination, dates, occasion and content type. An enquiry is not a booking and does not guarantee availability.

What photos or videos can I use?

Formats, resolution, duration and content rules depend on the screen. Wait for confirmed specifications before producing your final file. You need the necessary rights for images, music and people depicted.

How will I know the ad was shown?

We are checking which operators offer proof-of-play, photography or video, including costs and turnaround. A technical playback log and an HD video are different services: the quote should state exactly what is included.

Does CityBeam own the screens?

No. CityBeam is a Kreluna project to facilitate enquiries with authorised operators. Partnership and resale terms are being verified; we do not claim unconfirmed agreements.

The network is being built

We are speaking with operators.

CityBeam is not yet selling advertising space. We are verifying availability, resale terms, technical specifications and proof-of-play options with qualified operators.
