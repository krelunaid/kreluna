Before you decide.

How much does a Times Square ad cost?

Final CityBeam pricing is being defined. The price depends on the format, dates and requested services. Your quote should state the total, any taxes and what is included. Start by sharing your idea to request information.

Can I book or pay now?

Not yet. You can request information with your destination, dates, occasion and content type. An enquiry is not a booking and does not guarantee availability.

What photos or videos can I use?

Formats, resolution, duration and content rules depend on the screen. Wait for confirmed specifications before producing your final file. You need the necessary rights for images, music and people depicted.

How will I know the ad was shown?

We are clarifying playback confirmation, optional photography or video and their costs with the operator. These services are not automatically included: your proposal should specify what you will receive.

Does CityBeam own the screens?

No. The NY-D020 display shown on this page is operated by Heritage Outdoor. CityBeam is a Kreluna project; commercial and resale terms are still being defined. The photographs were provided with permission for use on this site.
