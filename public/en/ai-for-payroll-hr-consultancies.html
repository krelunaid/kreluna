Frequently asked questions

Does Kreluna provide payroll or employment advice?

No. Calculations, regulated interpretation and client actions require review by the appropriate specialist.

What is a suitable first workflow?

A recurring document process with clear owners, such as information intake, priority checks or draft communication.

How should employee data be protected?

Use restricted access, the minimum necessary data and agreed procedures before any real information is introduced.

Project method

An administrative workflow, not an employment decision

The first use case should avoid special-category data where unnecessary and clearly separate organisational support, professional processing and employer decisions.

  1. Define the outcome

    Describe the problem, who performs the work today and which observable result should improve.

  2. Map data and exceptions

    Identify sources, permissions, manual hand-offs, unusual cases and points where a person must decide.

  3. Test a narrow scope

    Run the pilot on authorised sample data against acceptance criteria agreed before the test.

  4. Measure and decide

    Compare the workflow with its baseline, then extend, revise or stop it on evidence rather than enthusiasm.

Data and accountability

Employee data and high-impact uses

Health, union or assessment information requires particular care. AI used for recruitment, management or employment decisions may fall under specific rules and is not included by implication.

  • Minimise data for each task
  • Separate employers, employees and processing periods
  • Exclude employment decisions from an administrative pilot
  • Keep approval for material changes
  • Verify provider, retention and transfers before use

Before you begin

What is not delegated to AI

Does Kreluna calculate payroll?

No. This page describes workflow support; payroll calculations and statutory work remain in authorised systems and professional control.

Can it make decisions about employees?

No. An administrative project excludes employment decisions. Such uses need a separate assessment and may be regulated as high risk.

How is special-category data protected?

The first control is not to use it unless necessary. Where required, purpose, access, security, retention and legal basis need competent assessment.

References for responsible design

The AI Act and GDPR can apply together: duties and safeguards depend on the organisation’s role, the data and the system’s actual use. Assessment therefore comes before configuration.

  • European Artificial Intelligence ActOfficial text of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.
  • EDPB Opinion 28/2024 on AI modelsAnonymity, legal basis and consequences of unlawful personal-data processing.
  • EDPB — Data protection by design and by defaultSME guidance on safeguards, minimisation and accountability.
  • OWASP — LLM Prompt Injection PreventionRisks and defences for AI applications connected to documents and tools.

These sources help frame the work; they do not replace legal, privacy or security advice for a specific situation.
