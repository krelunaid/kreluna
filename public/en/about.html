Project method

How an idea is assessed

A project passes through usefulness, feasibility, security and verifiability before becoming a presentable capability.

  1. Real problem

    The idea must map to observable work and a person accountable for the outcome.

  2. Explicit boundaries

    Data, prohibited actions, approvals and stop conditions are defined before a test.

  3. Declared prototype

    A demonstration remains a prototype or pilot until availability and support are confirmed.

  4. Publishable evidence

    Screenshots, integrations and outcomes enter the site only when real, authorised and understandable.
