What happens after the first message

The initial exchange establishes whether the request fits Kreluna’s scope. It does not imply availability, a quote or automatic activation.

  1. Read the context

    Separate the objective, urgency and missing information.

  2. Clarify scope

    If relevant, agree questions and a safe way to explore the request without unnecessary data.

  3. Assess feasibility

    Verify systems, constraints, responsibility and test criteria before a proposal.

  4. Propose or redirect

    Kreluna may suggest a route, request additional expertise or explain that the case is not suitable.
