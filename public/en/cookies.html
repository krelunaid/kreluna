Cookies and similar technologies

Kreluna stores a necessary preference to remember the visitor’s choice. The Meta Pixel loads only after explicit consent, and the website continues to work without marketing cookies.

Last updated: 25 August 2026.

Necessary storage

The local key kreluna-cookie-choice remembers whether measurement tools were accepted or rejected. It does not contain a name, email address or form content and remains in the browser until changed or deleted.

Optional Meta Pixel

After consent, the website may load Meta Pixel ID 1347647340858998 to measure page views and, on Velvet Table, a completed launch-list registration through the Lead event. The event does not send the email address entered in the form to Meta. Meta may process browser identifiers and technical information under its own terms and policies.

Meta Privacy Policy

Choice and withdrawal

Choose “Necessary only” or “Accept measurement” in the banner. You can change your choice at any time through “Cookie settings” on the main website pages. Refusal does not restrict access to content or the waitlist.

Technical logs

The hosting provider may generate logs required for infrastructure security, diagnosis and operation. These logs are separate from the Meta Pixel.
