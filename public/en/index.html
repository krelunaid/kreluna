Project method

How Kreluna approaches a first project

A useful engagement begins with the work, the information and the accountable person. Technology follows that definition.

  1. Describe the workflow

    State the trigger, participants, systems, exceptions and the outcome that matters.

  2. Set the boundaries

    Define authorised information, prohibited actions, approval points and stop conditions.

  3. Test a narrow example

    Use synthetic, minimised or otherwise authorised data with acceptance criteria agreed in advance.

  4. Publish only verified facts

    Availability, integrations, evidence and results are communicated only when they can be demonstrated.

What visitors should know

What is Kreluna?

Kreluna is an Italian project in development across AI, workflow automation and cybersecurity. Legal entity and tax details will be published when available.

Are the products generally available?

No general availability is claimed. Function scope, access and any commercial terms are confirmed for each request.

Does Kreluna replace existing systems or professional judgement?

No blanket replacement is proposed. The intended role is to connect and prepare work while approved systems and accountable professionals remain in control.
